Nearly 4 hours before the airport transfer. Use at your own pace. Two suggestions if you want structure:

Kitte building (3 min walk from hotel, next to Tokyo Station) — a restored 1930s post office building with excellent lunch restaurants on the basement and 6th floor, and a free rooftop garden with views over the Marunouchi skyline and Tokyo Station. A beautiful, calm, very Tokyo way to spend a final morning.

Tokyo Station Ramen Street (B1 of the First Avenue Tokyo Station) — eight ramen restaurants, each representing a different regional style. A bowl of Tokyo shoyu ramen — clear soy broth, thin noodles, chashu pork — is the correct final Japanese meal.
🍜 The last Japanese meal: pick ONE — either Ramen Street at 12:00 (arrive to beat the queue, 30–40 min wait at peak), or Kitte 6F for a sit-down lunch (less crowded, more relaxed). Don't try both — you'll have time to sit or walk afterward.

Boarding typically begins ~40 min before departure = 18:40. That leaves ~90 min airside for duty-free and any last purchases. Narita Terminal 1 has good dining options post-security if you skipped ramen earlier.

Duty-free is worth 30 minutes: Japanese whisky (Nikka, Suntory), matcha confectionery, MUJI airport store. These are the correct last Japan purchases.
🥃 Japanese whisky at Narita: Nikka From the Barrel and Suntory Toki are significantly cheaper in Japan than in Australia. Korea customs allows 1L alcohol duty-free per person. Worth checking prices before buying — some Narita duty-free is pricier than inner-city liquor stores.
💴 Leftover yen: don't exchange JPY → KRW at the airport — rates are poor. Spend remaining yen on airport food, whisky, or gifts. If you have ¥10,000+ left and won't return to Japan, ATMs at Narita can dispense USD or EUR if needed.

21:3570 min
Gimhae International → Grand Josun Busan Arrival₩40,000~A$44
Arrive Gimhae International Airport (PUS). Immigration → baggage claim → exit → taxi rank. Korean immigration is efficient, English-signed throughout. Realistic: ~35–45 min from landing to taxi rank. Hotel by 22:45–23:00.
🇰🇷 Gimhae → Grand Josun Busan
🚕
Taxi — the only reasonable choice at 22:30 with luggage. Metered taxi from the arrivals hall to Grand Josun Busan (Haeundae Beach) — ₩35,000–50,000 (~A$38–55), 35–45 minutes depending on traffic. Late evening Tuesday traffic is light. Taxis are metered and safe — no negotiation needed. Deluxe/International taxis (black with orange lettering) are fine; regular orange taxis also fine but drivers speak less English.
🏨
Grand Josun Busan check-in. Pre-paid, located directly at Haeundae Beach. Address: 292 Marine City 3-ro, Haeundae-gu, Busan (해운대해수욕장). Late check-in standard — front desk will have your reservation. Tell the taxi driver "그랜드 조선 부산" or show them the Hangul address. Bellhop service — ₩1,000–2,000 per bag gratuity is standard Korean luxury hotel practice (not required elsewhere in Korea).
💳
Korean currency — withdraw at airport ATM. Shinhan, KB Kookmin, or Woori Bank ATMs accept international cards with good rates. Withdraw ₩300,000–400,000 for first few days. Korea is less cash-dependent than Japan but street food, markets, and small restaurants still prefer it.
📱 Set up before leaving airport: (1) download Naver Maps — Google Maps is heavily crippled in Korea due to mapping regulations; Naver is essential. Alternative: KakaoMap. (2) Papago for translation (Korean-made, better than Google Translate for Korean). (3) Kakao T for taxi calls if needed later.
