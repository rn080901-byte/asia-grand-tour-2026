Shibuya Sky 🌆

Day 13B · Diamond Max Edition

Today is Tokyo at a slower, more cinematic pace. Instead of rides and queues, this route focuses on shrines, hidden streets, cafés, skyline views, fashion districts, and Tokyo after dark. The day builds slowly toward sunset at Shibuya Sky before transitioning into neon-lit Shinjuku nightlife.

Phase 1 · Early Morning
Meiji Shrine ⛩️
Tokyo begins quietly today. Before Shibuya neon, before crossings and skyline views, you walk through the cedar forest pathways of Meiji Shrine — one of the calmest places anywhere in the city.
⏰ Arrive ~07:30
Why Early Matters
By 09:30 the pathways begin filling with tour groups. At 07:30 the forest still feels silent and sacred.
Forest Walk
The approach through the towering cedar trees is the experience. Walk slowly. This is not a rushed sightseeing stop.
Diamond-Max Logic
The calm morning creates emotional contrast for the intensity of Shibuya and Shinjuku later tonight.
