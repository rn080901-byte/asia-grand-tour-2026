The Deliberate Pause

After Hiroshima — the most emotionally significant two days of the trip — Hakone is exactly what's needed. Mountain air, mineral water, silence, and two nights where the only obligation is to exist pleasantly. Today is the long journey to get there. The ryokan does the rest.

Transfer Day Logic

RealityHiroshima → Odawara is ~4 hours with a change. This is the longest train day of the Japan leg. Don't over-plan the morning.
RouteNozomi to Shin-Yokohama, change to Kodama or Hikari to Odawara. Or rare direct Hikari from Hiroshima if timetable aligns.
MountainHakone Tozan switchback railway from Hakone-Yumoto to Gora. Extraordinary engineering. Part of the experience, not just transport.
ArrivalLaforet check-in ~16:00. Unpack slowly. No agenda. First onsen before dinner. This is what the ryokan is for.
DinnerKaiseki — your half-board dinner. 7–9 seasonal courses. The finest meal of Japan. Served in the dining room or in-room depending on booking.

🚞 Phase 3 — Mountain Railway · Odawara → Gora

Before boarding the Tozan, stop at the Odakyu Sightseeing Service Centre at Odawara Station (west exit, clearly signed). Buy the Hakone Free Pass 2-day — ¥5,000pp. Covers the Tozan railway, cable car, ropeway, Lake Ashi pirate ship, and all local buses. Invaluable for tomorrow's full Hakone loop.
🎫 The Free Pass pays for itself halfway through tomorrow's loop. Paying per-leg for the ropeway + pirate ship + cable car would cost more than the pass covers. This is the right purchase.
14:0090 min
Hakone Tozan Railway — Odawara → Gora 🚞 Mountain TrainIncl. passFree Pass
Two segments. The first (Odawara → Hakone-Yumoto) runs on standard gauge track — 15 min, ordinary train. The second (Hakone-Yumoto → Gora) is the true Hakone Tozan mountain railway — narrow gauge, wooden seats, and the switchbacks that make it famous. This is the steepest adhesion railway in Japan — it reverses direction three times to climb the mountain.

~40 min · narrow gauge · 3× switchbacks · every ~20 min · sit on the right side for forest views

🏨
Gora Station → Laforet (via arranged shuttle)
Call Laforet from Hakone-Yumoto to confirm pickup · 5–10 min drive · saves uphill walk with daypacks after 4+ hours of travel
🌲 The switchbacks are the engineering highlight — the train stops, the driver and conductor walk to the opposite end of the carriage, and the train reverses up the next gradient. Watch for this happening. The landscape changes character as you climb — cedar forest, glimpses of valleys, distant mountains. The temperature drops 4–5°C by the time you reach Gora.

🏨 Laforet Hakone — What to Expect on Arrival
👘
Yukata robe — provided in the room. Wearable around the property and to the onsen. Modern Laforet properties are more relaxed about dinner dress than traditional ryokans; ask reception whether yukata is expected at dinner or whether smart casual is preferred.
🥿
Slippers throughout — remove shoes at the entrance. Slippers inside on wood; bare feet on tatami. There will be transition points signed.
🍽️
Kaiseki sitting time — confirm at check-in. Usually 18:00 or 18:30 for the first sitting, 19:30 for the second. Ask whether dinner is in-room or in the dining room (varies by room type).
♨️
Onsen schedule — public baths are gender-separated. Many ryokans swap male/female baths morning/evening. Ask which bath is for which time. If your room has a private rotenburo (outdoor bath), that's yours to use anytime.
🎭
Tattoo policy — if you confirmed this morning, you're set. If not, ask now. Small tattoos can usually be covered with provided bandages; large ones may restrict to private-bath only. Not a judgement; just local custom.
16:3090 min
First Onsen ♨️ OnsenIncl.Incl.
The onsen is the reason to stay at a ryokan. Hakone's volcanic water is rich in sulphates and sodium chloride, silky on the skin, typically 40–42°C. The outdoor bath (rotenburo) at 16:30 has the mountain views without the pre-dinner rush.
♨️ Onsen protocol: wash and rinse completely at the shower stations before entering the bath — non-negotiable. Remove all clothing and jewellery. Tie back long hair. Enter slowly; the water is hotter than it looks. Stay 10–15 minutes, exit, cool down, return if you want. The objective is not swimming — it's absorption.
💡 Afternoon 16:30–17:30 is the calmest onsen window. Guests have arrived but haven't yet started their pre-dinner bath routines. The outdoor bath in late-afternoon light, with cedar forest and mountain air, is one of the most restorative experiences of the entire 42-day trip.
18:302 hrs
Kaiseki Dinner — Half Board ⭐⭐ DinnerIncl.Incl.
Kaiseki is the pinnacle of Japanese cuisine — a multi-course meal built around seasonal ingredients, impeccable presentation, and the principle that every element arrives at its perfect moment. Your half-board includes this dinner. It is not something to rush.
🍱 Kaiseki — Typical Course Sequence
Sakizuke
Opening amuse-bouche — one or two bites. Sets the seasonal theme.
Hassun
The tray course — a selection of seasonal small dishes, usually including something from the sea and something from the mountain.
Mukōzuke
Sashimi — the freshest fish of the season. Often the most beautiful course visually.
Takiawase
Simmered vegetables and protein — quiet, deep-flavoured middle course.
Yakimono
Grilled course — usually fish or meat, simply prepared to show the ingredient.
Gohan
Rice, miso soup, pickles — the closing of the savoury meal.
Mizugashi
Dessert — usually fruit or a wagashi sweet. Light. The correct end.
🍶 Order a sake pairing or a single good local sake (nihonshu) — ¥800–1,500/glass. Staff will guide you. Hakone is close to Shizuoka, so regional sake options are available. The correct drink for kaiseki.
